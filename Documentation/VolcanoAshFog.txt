VOLCANO ASH FOG AND ATMOSPHERE

The saved Volcano_Demo automatically initializes the effect through its existing
VolcanoAtmospherePolish component. No scene rebuild or additional asset is needed.
The authored UI, opening/ending, input, evacuation, water and combat bindings are
left in place. No scene file or shared material/profile is modified by this work.

After the existing eruption begins, a six-second delay precedes a 48-second ash
front travelling down the actual terrain from the crater to the village. Six
rolling banks descend; six low banks gradually spread around the settlement.
They reuse the existing crater smoke material and its animation atlas in runtime
copies. All twelve systems together are limited to 192 particles. They have no
colliders, gameplay damage, shadow casting or additional probe lookups.

Distance fog develops with the front rather than appearing instantly. In the
settled storm it starts at 32 metres and reaches full density at 300 metres.
Shelter/room exposure adapts smoothly, keeping nearby paths, people and doors
readable. A weak cool sky bounce preserves house-front detail under the cloud
deck; a local, shadow-free bedroom window bounce supports the starting interior.
These are presentation lights; the authored sunny/volcanic sky transition and
existing lamps/lava lights retain their own settings.

VolcanoAtmospherePolish now owns distance fog while enabled.
VolcanoWeatherTransition keeps sunlight, sky and the native cloud deck, with its
old distance-fog behaviour available when the atmosphere component is absent or
disabled. The two components no longer overwrite each other's fog each frame.

Bloom uses a higher threshold and restrained intensity; vignette and chromatic
aberration are reduced. A subtle warm/cool grade and white-balance transition
separate the clear preparation from the ash-covered defense. Existing cinematic
depth of field stays limited to the cinematic volume; gameplay remains sharp.
All changed profile values live in runtime instances and are destroyed on unload.

The front reads VolcanoEruptionEffects.Elapsed. Pause and spirit-introduction
freezes therefore stop its travel. Restart resets the front, particles and grade.
The existing victory sequence keeps its original camera and gameplay timing.

Useful controls:
VolcanoAtmospherePolish: descendingFogEnabled, indoorExposureLift,
ashSkyBounceIntensity. The automatically created VolcanoDescendingAshFog exposes
startDelay, descentSeconds, nearVisibility and farVisibility during Play Mode.
