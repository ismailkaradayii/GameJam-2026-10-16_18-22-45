Truck guidance, lake hose and roof cannon fixes - 18 October 2026

Changes
- ObjectiveNavigation smooths the moving road target, world-arrow heading and HUD position/angle.
  HUD movement has a speed limit. Route/parking and road/shortcut transitions have hysteresis,
  so small position changes cannot repeatedly switch the destination display.
  Arrow edges are subdivided and lifted above the terrain to avoid disappearing through crests.
  The Lake distance label measures the actual lake destination, rather than the moving lookahead point.
- FireHoseSystem samples the terrain from above the whole hose, ignores the water surface as solid
  ground, accounts for hose thickness and checks the line between vertices at the steep bank.
  The actual scaled intake strainer is supported above the lakebed. If the authored connection is
  too shallow for its mesh, a nearby submerged placement is found in the same lake, within reach.
  The search uses the lake centre and covers all directions, including sideways parking.
  Stationary geometry is reused briefly to avoid repeating the full collision pass every frame.
  LakeFillStation retracts a failed connection rather than leaving a non-pumping intake deployed.
- FireTruckWaterCannon previously alternated nearly 180 degrees between successive frames when
  aiming near the barrel. Close targets now use one direction from the fixed pivot; distant
  targets retain the ballistic iteration. The rendered yaw and pitch rotate at up to 180 degrees
  per second. Prediction, packet emission and damage use the actual final muzzle pose.

Existing assets, terrain, truck scale/colliders, driving controls, pump controls, tank capacity,
water consumption, water-source validation and automatic retraction have been retained.
No scene or prefab authoring step is required. Runtime fixes apply when Play Mode starts.
