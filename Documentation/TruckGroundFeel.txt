Fire truck driving and wheel-ground presentation - 18 October 2026

The existing fire truck retains its WheelColliders, body mass, suspension, low-speed
turning assistance, hill hold and obstacle handling. Progressive handling shares a
smoothed steering input between visible wheels and assisted yaw. At road speeds the
turning circle opens according to a lateral-acceleration limit; yaw and service-brake
pressure build gradually. Parking, hill hold and wreck safety remain immediate.
Wheel spin from travel uses the scaled world wheel radius. Gear reversal drains
the old pedal under braking, preventing an old forward torque impulse when reversing
away from an obstacle below the usual direction-change speed threshold.

The authored truck uses 6000Nm per powered wheel, a 1.5 low-speed torque multiplier,
2m/s2 ground assistance, 104km/h forward and 26km/h reverse limits, 16000Nm service
brakes and 4m/s2 supplemental braking. Pedal response is 2.5/second with release at
5/second. Existing steering radius, suspension, collider sizes, centre of mass and
anti-roll settings are preserved. Progressive handling is opt-in for other trucks.

FireTruckGroundEffects samples the actual four wheel contacts. MudDry, Mud and
Mud_rubble accept impressions and soil dust; lava, water, buildings and fence surfaces
do not. Tire width comes from the wheel mesh. Mesh edges sample the terrain so marks
follow its height and slope. Connected tread strips break on contact loss, stop,
teleport, sharp surface changes and water crossings. Dust density follows rolling
speed, slip and braking, rising from the contact patches with a soft fade.

The existing Dust_a texture and Ground ash dust material are reused on a private
material instance; their source assets are unchanged. Two URP shaders provide tire
tread pattern, ambient/main-light shading, shadows and fog. Dust also softens depth
intersections. Explicit prefab material, shader and texture references include them
in standalone builds.

One bounded world-space mesh holds at most 4096 tread quads and one particle system
holds at most 480 dust particles. Marks fade over 80 seconds, with the oldest marks
reused earlier if the fixed buffer fills. No per-frame GameObject creation occurs.
Pause freezes both effects; disabling hides the owned render root and clears dust;
destroying the component releases its runtime objects, mesh and material instances.

GameJam > Final Loop > Apply truck ground feel authors the prefab and removes only
the selected driving overrides from the saved mission scene. Other unsaved scene
edits are copied to Library/TruckGroundFeelReview and preserved in the editor.
Check truck ground feel measures actual forces, contacts, tracks, dust, acceleration,
braking, turning, hill hold, reverse, pause, water, airborne wheels, teleport breaks,
bounded capacity and fading. It uses a temporary Terrain in Play Mode without
changing the source terrain. Reports and captures: Logs/TruckGroundFeel/.
Check truck lake handling checks the normal village/lake drive, filling and return
with the actual truck and terrain. It calls the real door entry/exit and lake pump
interaction methods directly; keyboard bindings/native editor focus are outside
this handling fixture. Run lake round trip retains the virtual-keyboard variant.
Both preserve the unsaved scene before Play Mode. Report: Logs/TruckLakeRoute-checks.txt.

Measured on the actual 6800kg truck: 0-50km/h in 4.16 seconds, peak forward
acceleration 6.69m/s2; service braking from 50.3km/h stopped in 1.38 seconds/10.94m.
The 9.7-degree test slope held with no drift and released into an uphill pull-away.
The final materials were checked against same-frame captures with effects disabled:
patterned impressions remain visible against the soil and rising dust changes more
than 10000 pixels above the track region in the low rear view.
The physical lake handling fixture completed 1099.3m in 106.57 seconds, reaching
the lake in 36.71 seconds, filling 2000L in 8.31 seconds and retaining it on return.
Peak speed was 64.0km/h; all colliders remained enabled and exit/re-entry succeeded.
