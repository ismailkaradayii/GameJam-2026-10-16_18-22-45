Combat view, pistol grip and soft aim - 18 October 2026

Truck monitor
- Raised the pitch pivot by 0.32 local units (approximately 0.79m in Volcano_Demo).
  The supporting riser is extended with its base unchanged, so the monitor stays attached.
- Downward travel is now 65 degrees for nearby creatures.
- RMB blends the rear driving camera to an elevated view over the cab. The height and forward
  offset follow the real truck collider dimensions. Mouse aim can look down to 85 degrees.
  This reveals targets just beyond the bumper instead of putting the long truck between them
  and the camera. Releasing RMB smoothly restores the previous driving pitch/orbit.

Pistol
- Hip position moved slightly toward the centre (.085 -> .055); sideways roll reduced (-6 -> -2).
- The right palm now follows the grip's rake (14 degrees more pitch around the thumb web),
  with the knuckles below the trigger guard. Its thumb stays along the frame and the lower
  three fingers curl around the grip instead of extending past it. Wrist-only lateral
  adjustment did not correct the previous floating-hand appearance.
  The same hand/weapon pose remains shared during ADS, recoil, locomotion and reload.
  PistolPolishSetup uses the same settings, so reapplying the presentation preserves this grip.
- PistolAimAssist is automatically connected by FireRescuePlayer. Holding RMB gently turns the
  view toward a live visible creature within 16 degrees / 75m. An acquired target is retained
  within 23 degrees, avoiding target switching in a crowd. Release/repress RMB to reacquire.
- Fast deliberate mouse movement overrides assistance. Walls, defeated targets, holstering,
  reloading, swimming, sprinting, vehicles, lost input capture, pause and cinematics suspend it.
  The component can be disabled to turn assistance off.
- Assistance adjusts the authoritative look angles before weapon presentation. Bullets still
  originate from the actual posed muzzle; obstruction, damage, ammo and manual firing remain physical.
